Getting Started

Follow these steps to set up size charts and size helpers for your products.

  1. 1
    Create a Size Template

    Go to Size Templates and click "Create Template". Choose your product category (tops, bottoms, shoes), enter your size measurements, and customize the appearance.

  2. 2
    Assign Products to Templates

    Visit the Products page to assign your size template to products. You can assign templates to individual products or use collection-based rules for bulk assignment.

  3. 3
    Add the Size Guide Block to Your Theme

    This step adds the "Size Guide" button to your product pages. Without it, the button won't appear even if a template is assigned.

    1. From your Shopify Admin, go to Online Store → Themes
    2. Click Customize on your current theme
    3. In the top-centre dropdown, select Products → Default product to open a product page template
    4. In the left sidebar, find the Product information section and click Add block
    5. Under Apps, select Size Guide
    6. Drag the block to position it where you'd like the button to appear (e.g. below the size selector or Add to Cart button)
    7. Click Save

    The Size Guide button will now appear on any product page that has a template assigned. Products without a template won't show the button.

  4. 4
    Track Performance

    Monitor how customers interact with your size guides on the Analytics page. See how many customers open guides and use the size recommendation feature.

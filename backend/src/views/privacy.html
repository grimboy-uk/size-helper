Privacy Policy

Last updated: January 27, 2026

Introduction

RMS Size Helper ("we", "our", or "the App") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, and safeguard information when you use our Shopify application.

Information We Collect

When you install and use RMS Size Helper, we collect the following information:

From Store Owners:

  • Shop Information: Your Shopify store domain and basic store details required for app functionality
  • Product Data: Product IDs, titles, and handles for products you assign to size templates
  • Size Templates: Custom size charts and measurement data you create within the app

From Store Customers:

  • Measurement Data: Body measurements entered voluntarily by customers when using the "Find My Size" feature
  • Size Preferences: Fit preferences and body shape selections made during size recommendations
  • Usage Analytics: Aggregated data on size guide views and recommendations made (no personal identifiers)

How We Use Information

We use the collected information to:

  • Provide personalized size recommendations to your customers
  • Display size charts on your product pages
  • Generate analytics and insights about size guide usage
  • Improve and maintain the App's functionality
  • Provide customer support

Data Storage and Security

All data is stored securely on encrypted servers. We implement industry-standard security measures to protect against unauthorized access, alteration, or destruction of your data.

  • Data is transmitted using SSL/TLS encryption
  • Database access is restricted and monitored
  • We do not sell or share your data with third parties for marketing purposes

Data Retention

We retain your data for as long as your App subscription is active. Analytics data (size guide opens and recommendations) is automatically deleted after 30 days. When you uninstall the App, your data is permanently deleted within 30 days.

Customer Data

Customer measurement data entered in the "Find My Size" questionnaire is used solely to provide size recommendations. This data is:

  • Not linked to customer accounts or personal identifiers
  • Not used for marketing or advertising purposes
  • Not shared with any third parties
  • Automatically deleted after 30 days

Your Rights

You have the right to:

  • Access: Request a copy of the data we hold about your store
  • Correction: Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Deletion: Request deletion of your data at any time
  • Portability: Request your data in a portable format

Third-Party Services

RMS Size Helper integrates with Shopify and uses Shopify's APIs. Your use of Shopify is governed by Shopify's Privacy Policy.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any significant changes by posting the new policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.

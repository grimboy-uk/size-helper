How Size Notation Conversion Works

  1. Choose measurement fields, unit, gender and measurement country.
  2. Assign matching measurements to each size.
  3. When a template is created, find a product to assign it to.
  4. When customers view the size guide popup, they can select their preferred country (US, UK, EU, etc.) from a dropdown.
  5. The popup displays gender specific information based on the selected gender of the template.
  6. The popup app will automatically display numeric equivalents next to the alphabetic sizes. For example: M (38) for EU or L (42) for EU.
  7. The Popup Button Colour you choose above will be used for all buttons inside the size guide modal on your storefront.
